# Decision NNN — [the decision, stated as an outcome not a topic]

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Status:** accepted | superseded by [NNN] | revisited [date]

## Context

What was happening that made this decision necessary. Where in the system, at what stage, prompted by what.

## Problem

The specific question that needed answering. One or two sentences. If you cannot state it as a question, the decision is not ready to be written.

## Constraints

What was not negotiable — time, tooling tier, team size, an earlier decision this one has to live with.

## Options

The alternatives that were genuinely considered. If there was only one option, this was not a decision and does not need an entry.

## Trade-offs

What each option costs. Be honest about the cost of the option that won — an entry where the chosen option has no downsides is not being truthful.

## Decision

What was decided. Stated plainly, in one or two sentences.

## Why

The reasoning. This is the section a reader is actually here for.

## Consequences

What is now true that was not true before. Include the unwelcome consequences; they are the ones that matter later.

## What would make us revisit this?

The concrete signal that would reopen this. "If X happens, this decision is wrong." An entry without a revisit trigger is a preference, not a decision.
